## Current Status

**Stage:** Behavior-first product redesign and implementation  
**Primary objective:** Turn Kairos into a calm personal discipline system for time management, career compounding, and life balance  
**Current focus:** Simplify the user loop so Today, Weekly, Focus, Review, Brain, Research, and Coach work together instead of feeling like separate tools

## Current Product Thesis

Kairos should help the user:

- know what matters
- choose what to do now
- start when motivation is low
- protect focus from internal and external triggers
- turn misses into useful feedback
- improve weekly planning discipline over time

## Core Loop

1. North Star and Areas define what matters.
2. Goals turn direction into outcomes.
3. Weekly turns outcomes into realistic capacity commitments.
4. Today turns the week into one next action.
5. Focus turns the action into timed execution.
6. Check-in captures result, friction, quality, mood, and energy when useful.
7. Review turns evidence into the next adjustment.

## What Is Completed

- Weekly planning, weekly plan storage, allocation, and rollover.
- Review planned-vs-actual metrics, focus heatmap, and decision-first summary.
- Post-session quality, mood, and energy fields.
- Activity session types.
- Brain questionnaire storage, synthesis, and Obsidian sync.
- Research search/read/save flow with local SearXNG.
- Playwright review of Today, North Star, Areas, Focus, Goals, Weekly, Review, Research, Brain, and Coach.
- Behavior-first product blueprint and docs consistency pass.

## What Is Next

1. Simplify Today into a stronger Now-first command center.
2. Merge Today checklist and Daily Discipline into one interactive loop.
3. Make Weekly more guided and realism-aware.
4. Make Review lead with one lesson and one adjustment.
5. Make North Star read-first.
6. Add contextual Coach prompts on decision-heavy screens.
7. Keep Brain and Research as the local memory layer rather than generic capture surfaces.
