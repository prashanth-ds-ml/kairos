# Page Goals

## Today
- act as the command center
- surface one clear recommended next block
- keep today's work intentionally small

## Focus
- feel immersive and emotionally distinct
- minimize noise during active sessions
- make transitions feel deliberate

## Dashboard
- interpret progress, not just display it
- show momentum, consistency, and goal health

## Goals
- feel strategic, not administrative
- show meaning, progress, and next recommended work

## History
- work as a quiet readable record
- prioritize scanability over density
