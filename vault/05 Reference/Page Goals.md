# Page Goals

## Today

Primary question: What should I do now?

- act as the daily command center
- surface one clear recommended next action
- keep today's work intentionally small
- merge morning setup, commitments, focus progress, and shutdown into one loop

## Focus

Primary question: What am I doing for this block?

- show selected target and timer first
- reduce noise during active sessions
- capture complete, partial, blocked, friction, quality, mood, and energy
- support Activity mode separately from Focus mode

## Weekly

Primary question: What is realistic this week?

- start with real capacity
- allocate capacity to top goals
- carry rollover intentionally
- warn when the plan is too ambitious

## Review

Primary question: What happened and what should change?

- lead with planned vs actual
- show best goal, neglected area, main friction, and suggested adjustment
- keep detailed charts below the lesson
- make misses useful instead of shameful

## Brain

Primary question: What have I learned about myself?

- capture questionnaire answers and synthesize them into editable memory
- stay honest, reflective, and non-diagnostic
- connect raw answers to North Star, Goals, Areas, and Research

## Goals

Primary question: What outcome am I moving and what is the next action?

- feel strategic, not administrative
- show goal progress, active task, and weekly commitment
- expose health warnings later

## Areas

Primary question: Is my life balanced enough?

- show area score, target, actual investment, and status
- identify one area needing attention
- keep editing secondary

## North Star

Primary question: Why does this matter?

- show identity, season focus, 90-day outcomes, and priorities
- stay read-first
- support monthly or quarterly review

## Coach

Primary question: Where am I stuck and what is the next move?

- suggest from Kairos data
- stay concise and non-judgmental
- appear contextually on decision-heavy screens
- continue working even when Hugging Face is unavailable
